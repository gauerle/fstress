- FTS stands for Financial Threat Scale, proposed by [[@marjanovic2013]]
- Self-reported [[Financial threat|financial threat]] construct
- Rate items on five point scales
- It has 5 items that address uncertainty, risk, perceived threat, [[Financial preoccupation|financial preoccupation]] with one's personal finances
