- FSS-CV stands for Financial Stress Scale-College Version, by [[@northern2010]]
- 12 items on five point scales: 1 (never) -> 4 (all the time)
- Self-reported [[Financial stress|financial stress]] construct with three factors, representing:
	- Credit score-related stressors
	- Inability to endure unpredictable financial emergencies
	- Constraints to financial situation
- Besides the name, authors claim it can be used in any population
