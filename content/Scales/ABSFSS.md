- ABSFSS stands for Australian Bureau of Statistics [[Financial stress|Financial Stress]] Scale, proposed by [[@bray2001]]
- Rate items on different point scales
- It has 13 questions divided in the following domains:
	- Missing out, i.e. financial stress is reflected in the extent to which households are missing out on fully participating in daily life because of money constraints
	- Cashflow problems, i.e. being unable to properly manage financial activities
	- Hardship, i.e. due to a lack of money, the household had to go through or cope with extreme actions![[Pasted image 20250829222323.png]]
