- APRFSS stands for Affective-Physiological-Relational Financial Stress Scale, proposed by [[@heo2020]]
- Rate items on different point scales
- It has 24 questions divided in the following domains that constitute the higher-level [[Financial stress|financial stress]] latent variable:
	- Affective reaction
	- Relational or interpersonal behavior
	- Physiological responses
