- EEF stands for Estrés Financiero en Empresarios, proposed by [[@tito-betancur2021]]
- Self-reported [[Financial stress|financial stress]] construct with one factor 
- It has 11 items (Likert type, 1--5) to evaluate stress level and common complaints as consequence of economic effects of COVID-19
