- FWR stands for [[Financial preoccupation|Financial Worry and Rumination]] questionnaires, proposed by [[@debruijn2020]] using part of the [[FR]] scale
- 5-point Likert-type scale ranging from 1 (completely disagree) to 5 (completely agree)
- It has 20 questions divided in four blocks:
	- Financial-rumination-related emotions
	- Financial-rumination-related cognitions
	- Financial worry-related emotions
	- Financial worry-related cognitions
