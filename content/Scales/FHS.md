- FHS stands for [[Financial hardship|Financial Hardship]] Scale, validated by [[@surachman2023]] and proposed by [[@tucker-seeley2019]]
- It has 11 indicators (variable answer type) that reflects three different factors:
	- Psychological (subdivided in financial satisfaction and [[Financial stress|financial stress]]) domain
	- Material domain
	- Behavioral domain
