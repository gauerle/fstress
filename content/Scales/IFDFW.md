- IFDFW stands for Financial Distress/Financial Well-Being, developed by [[@prawitz2006]]
- Composite scores range from 1 (overwhelming [[Financial distress|financial distress]]) to 10 (no financial distress)
- It has 8 questions representing the sense of an individual's current financial state as well as the sentiment towards his own financial state
