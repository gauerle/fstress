- FSS stands for Financial Strain Scale, proposed by [[@aldana1998]]
- Self-reported [[Financial strain|financial strain]] construct
- Rate items on five point scales: 1 (never) -> 5 (always)
- It has 18 items divided in domains:
	- Education
	- Relationships
	- Physical
	- Credit card use
	- Meeting obligations
