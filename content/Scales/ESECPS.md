- EEFCCS stands for Escala de Estrés Financiero en Empresarios en Contextos de Conflictos Sociales, proposed by [[@tito-betancur2023]]
- Self-reported [[Financial stress|financial stress]] construct with one factor 
- It has 9 (Likert type, 1--5) items to evaluate stress level and common complaints as consequence of economic effects of COVID-19
