- COST stands for Comprehensive Score for Financial Toxicity
- Includes questions assessing degree of [[Financial toxicity|financial toxicity]] in patients with advanced cancer diagnoses
- Created by [[@desouza2014]] and validated for US cancer patients; the construct originally formed a single factor (on finances, resources, affects, coping and effects on family)
	![[Pasted image 20250830051036.png|400]]
- Validated by [[@sharif2020]] for Persian cancer patients; the construct changed to three dimensions (financial worry, financial control and direct-indirect costs concerns) and had 11 items:
