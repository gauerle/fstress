- EHQ stands for Economic Hardship Questionnaire, proposed by [[@lempers1989]]
- Self-reported [[Economic hardship|economic hardship]] construct
- Rate items on 4-point scales (1--4)
- It has 10 items that assess the extent to which the respondent has had to make cutbacks to his or her lifestyle due to financial difficulties
