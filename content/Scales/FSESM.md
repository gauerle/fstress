- FSESM stands for Financial Strain and Economic Support Measure, proposed by [[@krause1998]]
- Rate items on different point scales
- It has three questions in the chronic [[Financial strain|financial strain]] domain:
	- Does your family have enough for daily living expenses each month?
	- Compared to other people your own age, how do you feel about your financial situation?
	- Do you have enough pocket money to spend?
