- PIFS stands for Psychological Inventory of Financial Scarcity, proposed by [[@vandijk2022]]
- Four-factor construct measuring [[Financial scarcity]]
- 5-point Likert-type scale ranging from 1 (very rarely) to 5 (very often)
- It has 12 questions divided in four domains:
	- SoM: shortage of money, insufficient financial resources
	- LoC: lack of control over one's financial situation
	- RW: responses of financial rumination and worry
	- STF: short-term focus on one's finances
