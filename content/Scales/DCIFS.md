- DCIFS stands for Dyadic Coping Inventory for [[Financial stress|Financial Stress]], proposed by [[@falconier2019]]
- 5-point Likert-type scale ranging from 1 (very rarely) to 5 (very often)
- It has 23 questions divided in six blocks:
	- Stress communication
	- Emotion-focused supportive dyadic coping
	- Emotion-focused common coping
	- Problem-focused supportive dyadic coping
	- Problem-focused common coping
	- Negative dyadic coping
