| Processes                     | Measures                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Information asymmetry         | Credit spreads, funding spreads, foreign exchange market, and banking market                                                         |
| Fundamental value uncertainty | Financial volatility, foreign exchange market, equity valuations, and stock market return, real estate                               |
| Behavioral uncertainty        | Financial volatility, equity market, and foreign exchange market                                                                     |
| Flight to liquidity           | Money market, foreign exchange market, interest rates, securitization, and funding spreads                                           |
| Flight to quality             | Safe assets, security market, foreign exchange market, sovereign debt, sovereign market, bond market, debt market and credit spreads |
| Concerns on banking health    | Banking market, foreign exchange market, financial intermediares, and funding spreads                                                |
