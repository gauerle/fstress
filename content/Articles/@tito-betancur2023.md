> After analyzing the Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin index (KMO = 0.962) and Bartlett’s test (5434.3, df = 55, p < 0.001), which yielded satisfactory results, an EFA was conducted. The unweighted least squares method with promin oblique rotation was used, and for factor determination, parallel analysis was used, which revealed the existence of a single factor underlying the 11 items. The rotated solution explains 77.10% of the total explained variance.





> [!quote]- tito-betancur2023 - [p. 4](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MQAEVU67?page=4&annotation=M5FFCJWI)
