Respondents were recruited and paid by a research  company that maintains an online access panel.2 To obtain a representative sample, invited participants had to first answer questions on their sociodemographic characteristics.





> [!quote]- jeworrek2025 - [p. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/49Y8SRFW?page=3&annotation=9539NVU8)
> The dependent variable, FinancialConcernsi, contains the answer with respect to respondent i’s concerns about the current (or future) financial situation which ranges from “not at all concerned” (1) to “very concerned” (7). Our main variable of interest, InflationConcernsi, has three categories, whereas the category not at all concerned is the reference category.





> [!quote]- jeworrek2025 - [p. 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/49Y8SRFW?page=3&annotation=PEQZFKY9)
> The more concerned respondents are about inflation, the more they are stressed about the current financial situation. Importantly, this holds when controlling for inflation expectations, income, and financial constraints.
