Branching from the [[Cognitive-transactional stress theory]], this framework proposes stress as a result of a transaction of information between a couple, its parts and their environment. Guy Bodenmann (1995) argues that a dyad constitutes a system, i.e. both parts have an impact on each other reciprocally. This means individual stress can become a dyadic stress when shared.
