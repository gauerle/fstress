Proposes that early advantages and disadvantages aggregate over time to impose different trajectories by increasing, respectively, opportunities and risk exposure. The inequality generated in social systems influence later life outcomes since childhood, changing access to resources and influencing human agency, as well as its perception. 

Branches from [[Cumulative disadvantage theory]].

Developed by Kenneth Ferraro and Tetyana Shippee (2009).
