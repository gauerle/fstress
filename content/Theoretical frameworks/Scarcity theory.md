The core mechanism of this theory is that having less leads to greater focus. As focus on specific problems increases, there is decreased engagement in other equally important tasks. Attentional neglect extends through time, in the sense that the consequences of current task (e.g. pressing expenses today) aren't taken into account. This way, scarcity creates a tendency to borrow.

Proposed by Anuj Shah in 2012.
