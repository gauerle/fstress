Suggests that LGBTQ individuals face stigma, prejudice and discrimination-based stressors in addition to the stressors of daily living experienced by all, leading to expectations of rejection, concealment, internalized homophobia and negative mental health outcomes. This process, though, can be moderated by social support and minority identity. 

Initially proposed by Ilan Meyer (1995). Have close relationship with the [[Stress process theory]].
