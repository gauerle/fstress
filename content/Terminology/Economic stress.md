## Definitions
- Aspects of economic life that are potential stressors for employees and their families, consisting of objective (employment instability; economic deprivation) and subjective (employment uncertainty; [[Economic strain|economic strain]]) facets of the worker-earner role ([[@voydanoff1990]], [[@sinclair2010]], [[@probst2005]])
- The response to a real or perceived danger to one’s standard of living/finances ([[@rosso2024]])
- The pressures and strains that arise from a substancial income loss, in contrast to the circumstances of chronic hardship or poverty ([[@elder1988]])
