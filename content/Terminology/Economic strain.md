## Definitions
- Perception and evaluation of one's current financial status, as one of the subjective components of [[Economic distress|economic distress]] ([[@voydanoff1990]], [[@probst2005]], [[@sinclair2010]], [[@sinclair2016]])
- The negative psychological and emotional responses due to insufficient resources to deal with a real or perceived threat to one’s standard of living/finances ([[@rosso2024]])
- Experience of [[Financial hardship|financial hardship]] ([[@aldana1998]])
