## Definitions
- Concept referring to aspects of economic life that are potential stressors for individuals and families, which are: employment instability, employment uncertainty, economic deprivation, and economic strain ([[@voydanoff1990]])
- The negative response due to insufficient resources to deal with a real or perceived threat to one’s standard of living/finances ([[@rosso2024]])

## Theoretical frameworks
- [[Stress process theory]]:
	- [[@voydanoff1990]]: Recapitulates that the processes and factors intervening between a stressor and individual and family outcomes vary according to the scope
- [[Double ABCX theory]]:
	- [[@voydanoff1990]]: remembers the conditions under which stressors are associated with family crisis or disrupted family functioning
