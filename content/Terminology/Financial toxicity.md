## Definitions
- Patient-reported outcome of perceived subjective financial burden ([[@witte2019]], [[@sharif2020]], [[@zhu2022]], [[@drummo2022]])

## Determinants

#### Social network
- [[@drummo2022]]: in patients with cancer, greater social network support lead to decreased financial toxicity (β=8.5), controlling for age, savings and household income
