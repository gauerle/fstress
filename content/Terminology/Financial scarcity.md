## Definitions
- Having fewer financial resources than needed ([[@vandijk2022]])
---
## Theoretical frameworks
- [[Cognitive-transactional stress theory]]:
	- [[@vandijk2022]]: solidify the importance of the subjective perception of a financial situation
- [[Scarcity theory]]:
	- [[@vandijk2022]]: suggests the accompanying affective and cognitive responses to stressors are related to focus and neglect behaviors

---

## Outcomes
#### Self-esteem
- [[@vandijk2022]]: financial scarcity lead to decreased self-esteem (β=-0.19), controlling for gender, age, education, income and personality
#### Mental health
- [[@vandijk2022]]: financial scarcity lead to impaired mental health (β=-0.10), controlling for gender, age, education, income and personality
#### Life satisfaction
- [[@vandijk2022]]: financial scarcity lead to decreased life satisfaction (β=-0.31), controlling for gender, age, education, income and personality
