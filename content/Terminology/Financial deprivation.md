## Definitions
- Objective assessments of one's current level of financial inputs, debts or resources; also calls it economic deprivation ([[@sinclair2016]])
- Unable to afford social activities ([[@yates2007]])

---

## How it's measured

#### Ad-hoc measures
- [[@sinclair2016]] suggests different objective parameters, such as:
	- Direct income (from job, from household)
	- Adjusted income (demand-adjusted income, relative income rank)
	- Financial obligations (mortgage, debts, expenses, proxies such as number of children or spousal employment status)
- [[@yates2007]] uses data collected from HES, on deprivation in:
	- 'Week's holiday away from home at least once a year'
	- 'Night out once a fortnight'
	- 'Special meal once a week'
	- 'New clothes'
	- 'Leisure or hobby activities'
	- 'Inviting family/friends for a meal once a month'
